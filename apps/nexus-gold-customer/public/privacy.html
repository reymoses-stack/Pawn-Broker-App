Privacy Policy for Nexus Gold Customer

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Welcome to Nexus Gold Customer. Your privacy and the security of your financial and personal information are of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and safeguard your data when using the mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification: Name, mobile number, and contact details provided when requesting gold loan estimates or doorstep evaluations.
  • Loan & Pledge Information: Pawn ticket numbers, loan reference identifiers, and pledge balances requested through the digital passbook.
  • Device & Usage Data: Basic device telemetry for crash prevention and biometric security authentication.

2. Purpose of Data Use

  • To calculate live loan valuations based on prevailing bullion market rates.
  • To deliver digital passbook records and live payment receipts.
  • To process doorstep pawn appraisal and booking requests.
  • To comply with regulatory anti-money laundering (AML) and KYC statutory requirements.

3. Security & Data Protection

All sensitive communications between the mobile application and backend services are encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to inspect, correct, or request deletion of your profile data in compliance with applicable financial laws by contacting our customer support team.
